# AI Assistance and Human Responsibility

[Portfolio](../README.md) · [Foundations](foundations.md)

These projects were developed with substantial AI assistance, including Codex and other AI systems. The work should not be interpreted as having been produced without those tools.

## How AI contributed

The project records describe AI assistance with research synthesis, requirements analysis, architecture alternatives, implementation, test creation, documentation, review, debugging, and evidence preparation. Different records have different approval and evidence states; assistance does not make an output accepted or correct.

This portfolio itself was drafted and reviewed with AI assistance from the underlying project documents.

## Human responsibility

The accountable human defines goals, authorizes scope and resources, decides material architecture and risk questions, and retains approval authority for merges, releases, and consequential use. AI systems do not acquire those powers by producing persuasive text or successful test output.

Human-led work here includes choosing the questions, setting boundaries, challenging results, deciding what evidence is sufficient, and controlling whether work proceeds. This repository does not assert that every implementation line was manually authored or independently inspected by a human.

## How confidence is earned

The intended review method combines deterministic checks, bounded experiments, retained failures, independent evidence, and human decisions. Multiple AI reviews do not by themselves establish independent corroboration or security assurance.

Claims remain tied to what the available evidence actually shows. Production readiness, research novelty, general safety, and commercial value each require their own proof.
