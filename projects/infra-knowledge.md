# Infra Knowledge

**Purpose:** investigate whether structured expression and evidence-centered records can preserve intended meaning across people, AI systems, software, and execution.

[Portfolio](../README.md) · [Research findings](../docs/research-and-findings.md) · [Architecture](../docs/architecture.md)

## The problem

A handoff can preserve plausible words or valid data while losing an important relationship: which evidence supports the claim, which authority controls the action, whether a record is still current, or whether the work still serves its parent objective.

The project investigates those failures within internal project-governance and technical-knowledge work. That internal evidence does not establish external market demand or general superiority of controlled language.

## Conceptual direction

The design separates structured meaning, human-readable presentation, evidence and provenance, temporal state, and checks before action. A valid structure must not silently become accepted knowledge or permission to execute.

The project compares stronger prose practices with structured records and deterministic enforcement. A bespoke language or a particular database is not assumed to be the solution simply because it is technically available.

## Documented progress

Later project records place the work in Design, beyond the historical Define-era summary still present in the root README. A conceptual design consolidation and several local synthetic experiments are documented.

Those experiments examine action gating, structural admission, preservation in generated human-facing views, and conditional resolution of authority and decision relevance. They include frozen expected outcomes, repeatability checks, failed checkpoints, repairs, and explicit unsupported or unresolved cases.

The integrated Design-to-Verify gate remains not ready in the reviewed reconciliation. Experimental implementations do exist; a complete verified system does not.

## Selected findings

- Initial fixtures passing did not prevent later boundary tests from revealing defects.
- Structural admission and byte integrity did not establish correct meaning or actual authority.
- A generated view could preserve much of the source while still mishandling a material permission or reference binding.
- Local work could remain mechanically valid after its parent decision need had changed.
- Provenance references and operative authority had to remain distinct to avoid misleading attribution.

The [research page](../docs/research-and-findings.md) states which observations came from internal incidents, which came from synthetic experiments, and which remain hypotheses.

## Remaining questions

Integrated interpretation agreement, genuine authority and rights validation, provider effects, durable custody, migration behavior, human understanding, and complete security and verification evidence remain open. No experiment here establishes a general safety rate, production permission system, or universal test of semantic equivalence.
